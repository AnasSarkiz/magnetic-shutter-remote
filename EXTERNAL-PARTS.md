# R8 external product parts

- Battery: retained TinyCircuits **ASR00012**, protected1S1000mAh,
  maximum43×32×8.5mm, JST SHR-02V-S-B. Verify J2 pin1GND/pin2positive.
- Programmer cable: straight-through three-pin JST SH cable, SHR-03V-S
  housings, standard JST programmer0.8.0 **J5→R8J3**; no target power contact.
- Printed parts: remote base/lid, battery perimeter support, horizontal shutter
  plunger and detachable grip/cradle. [Native assembly and print files](product/README.md).
- Four PCB/lid fasteners: M2 nylon hardware at the existingØ2.2mm holes.
  Exact hardware/thread retention and assembly sequence need qualification.
- MagSafe: exact magnet array and its required DC shield remain unselected.
  The CAD annulus is an Apple R31 dimensional reference, not a purchased or
  qualified magnet. Do not order magnets from this envelope alone.
- Battery leads, insulation/strain relief and sensor-to-cell thermal coupling
  need final physical specification. The model reserves space; it does not
  qualify an unspecified thermal pad or cable bend radius.

No RGB light/connector is fitted. No historical steel phone plate/four-disc
magnet arrangement is required. Ordering/payment/supplier contact require
separate authorization. Original external-parts text is retained in the
[history archive](archive/legacy-mechanics-and-views-2026-10-07.tar.gz).
