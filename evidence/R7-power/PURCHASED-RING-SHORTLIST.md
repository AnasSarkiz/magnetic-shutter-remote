## Manufacturer evidence

- [Adafruit 5138](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5138): power/current table, fixed white, PWM and heat warning. Power-only integration is possible in principle but exact reduced-power performance is not documented here.
- [Adafruit 2855](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2855): assembled/tested RGBW ring, dimensions, serial brightness control.
- [Adafruit 2862](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2862) and [manufacturer product document](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/2379/2862_Web.pdf): 5 V / serial protocol; approximately 400 Hz PWM stated in historical document. Product revisions must be identified before binding firmware. This may produce camera banding; physical phone exposure tests remain pending, not presumed failures or passes.
- [Manufacturer ring guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-neopixel-uberguide/advanced-coding?view=all): RGBW white variants have fixed approximately 3000/4500/6000 K white emitters. Adding RGB to a fixed-white ring does not establish CRI ≥90 or qualified 2500/5500/9000 K output.

No option above has been shown to meet all previously accepted photography requirements. Do not silently relax those requirements. The 2855 is the most compact inexpensive digital-control experiment among these, not a claimed final solution. The 5138 is the cheapest power-only connection but has explicit power/heat and color limitations. Exact supply, connector and battery sizing remain unselected.
