Original inputs and upstream defects remain explicit. 077 concerns downloaded error bodies; selected assets were format-checked. 080 affects exact-MPN supplier searches; identity must be verified against exact C-number responses and listings rather than accepting unrelated results.

078: TI's drawing is an example PCB pattern. The supplier contacts are 0.665 × 0.280 mm versus 0.600 × 0.250 mm, with row centres ±1.157478 versus ±1.150 mm. Imported inner/outer edges 0.8249793 / 1.4899767 mm extend the example's 0.850 / 1.450 mm by 0.0250207 mm at heel and 0.0399767 mm at toe. This retains contact coverage; greater copper is not by itself an electrical failure. Minimum adjacent copper gap 0.2198624 mm exceeds the 0.15 mm board requirement. Explicit 0.0508 mm mask expansion leaves 0.1182624 mm web, above 0.1 mm. EP/contact copper separation 0.2249805 mm and mask web 0.1233805 mm also exceed these limits. EP 1.2 × 2.0 mm matches the example. A native board solder-paste margin of -0.0625 mm gives 1.075 × 1.875 mm aperture, 83.984% area coverage. At an assumed 0.1 mm stencil its area ratio is 3.416 (>0.66). This is prototype process selection, not TI approval or a reason to edit the generated import. Full routed copper/thermal connectivity and manufacturing output still require checks.

079: Sunlord's recommended 0.8 × 2.7 mm lands / 1.5 mm gap differ from supplier 1.2999974 × 2.7200098 mm lands / 1.4000226 mm gap. Actual terminal 0.75 ± 0.2 mm, body 3.0 ± 0.2 mm retain terminal overlap with larger symmetric supplier lands and ample inter-pad clearance. Extra toe area alone is not a short or proof of inability to assemble. Paste aperture area ratio at assumed 0.1 mm stencil is about 4.40 (>0.66). Prototype acceptance retains the supplier footprint; reflow, solder volume and production capability need assembly inspection. Electrical: 1.5 µH ±30%, DCR max 0.065 Ω, saturation 2.3 A, rated RMS 1.7 A per inspected manufacturer table. These support the TI 1.5 µH buck-boost application, subject to transient/thermal hardware tests.

## Circuit requirements

- TPS63031 fixed 3.3 V, VIN/VINA to VBAT, FB to VOUT, PS/SYNC to ground for power saving; EP/PGND/GND grounded. 2 × 10 µF nominal 25 V X5R input and 2 × 10 µF nominal output provide derating headroom. This report does not claim a manufacturer DC-bias curve was obtained. VINA has a separate 100 nF bypass. Real transient response remains physical validation.
- Module includes EN 10 kΩ/1 µF reset network and GPIO8/9 boot pull-ups (actual DOIT C3 schematic printed page15, PDF18). GPIO2 also retains its internal pull-up; no external boot-strap load is connected. No redundant external network. BOOT switch drives GPIO9 low, RESET switch drives EN low. Boot requires GPIO8 high and GPIO9 low across EN rising, with 3 ms strap hold; GPIO8 remains unused externally. ESP32-C3 reset/strap contract requires supply settle and reset pulse each ≥50 µs.
- Module uses ESP32-C3 / 4 MB flash. Firmware targets ESP32-C3 at40 MHz per silicon requirement and the qualified pinned target. UART GPIO20 RX and GPIO21 TX use3.3 V logic. The manufacturer schematic does not print a crystal-frequency value; actual oscillator operation remains prototype verification. Historical C2/Nordic binaries cannot be used. J3 pin1 is voltage reference only: do not inject supply. Pins2 RX,3 GND,4 TX,5 EN,6 BOOT, shells grounded.
- User pins GPIO4 shutter, GPIO5 pair, GPIO6 status LED. Active-low buttons need firmware pull-ups.
- Existing BQ25185 USB charge, temperature window and battery cutoff retained. R3 becomes exact C21190 1 kΩ for nominal 300 mA; tolerance range 267.3–333.3 mA with ±10% charger / ±1% resistor. Battery's 500 mA standard charge limit exceeds worst case. Existing 130 kΩ VSET selects 4.1 V with 4.1205 V upper tolerance below 4.20 V battery limit. 6 h safety timer versus ideal 1 Ah/0.3 A =3.33 h leaves time for CV; hardware must verify actual termination and thermal derating. Worst simple charger dissipation (5.5−3.0)×0.3333≈0.833 W needs physical thermal validation. USB disables the radio regulator so Wi-Fi/BLE load is not added to charging current.
- Battery SH connector C160402 pin2 positive, pin1 ground, shells grounded, following actual selected pack contract. One battery, one USB port, no torch.
- Radio rotated180 at (0,-10.5) places antenna body to about Y=-26.875. Entire all-layer board band Y[-28,-19.3] excludes routing, pours, other parts and battery; side-pad centres start at about-18.375. DOIT antenna solution3 uses antenna PCB area empty/no copper. Battery below PCB at (0,6), 43 ×32 ×8.5 mm envelope, extends Y[-10,22]. Mounts x±21/Y25,-14 avoid the pack and antenna. Actual enclosure/fastener/pack fit remains physical validation.
