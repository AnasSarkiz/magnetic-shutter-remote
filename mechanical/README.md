# R8 product mechanics

Current native product assembly is in [product/](../product/README.md).
Use `product.assembly.tsx` for the closed product and `product.exploded.tsx`
for the exploded view. Both use the actual routed44-component R8 board.

[MagSafe requirements](R8-MAGSAFE-REQUIREMENTS.md), [research](R8-MAGSAFE-RESEARCH.md)
and [reference product review](R8-MAGSAFE-REFERENCE-REVIEW.json) remain current inputs.

The old Nordic enclosure, drawings, STL files, battery selection and phone-test
procedure are historical. Their exact original bytes are recoverable from
[the verified archive](../archive/legacy-mechanics-and-views-2026-10-07.tar.gz).
They are not R8 fit or functional evidence. The immutable baselines remain intact.
