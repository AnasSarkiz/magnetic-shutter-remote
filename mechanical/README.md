# Original remote and grip — R6

This is an original design, not a replacement for JJC internals. Dimensions and provenance are in [dimensions.json](dimensions.json) and [current R6 drawing](../fabrication/R6/BOARD-DIMENSIONS.pdf). The editable OpenSCAD source and seven exported STL part types are included.

PCB: 36 × 56 × 1 mm, R2 corners, two layers, all 37 fitted electronics TOP. Remote body: 40 × 60 × 16.8 mm, R3, 1.2 mm walls; maximum slider width 42.4 mm and button height 17.5 mm. Grip: 68 × 108 mm, R16; base thickness 6.5 mm, overall height with rails 9.8 mm. Docked remote body reaches Z=23.6 mm, buttons Z=24.3; the external steel plate reaches Z=-0.5, so the plate-to-button stack is 24.8 mm. These are compact engineering allocations around qualified parts, not a proven global minimum size.

Board datum: XY centre, +Y toward USB; outer back Z=0, PCB bottom 5 and top 6. Ø2.2 mounting holes are at (±13,24). HCTL USB anchor (0,23.25), 180°, mates +Y. Mouth Y=28.0601694; front opening 12 × 7 at (0,29,8). Its 3.31 mm maximum body height leaves 2.19 mm upper opening clearance at nominal board height. Both plug orientations have independent CC pull-downs. Intended cable plug envelope 10 × 6.5 is an allocation, not a guarantee for every cable.

Battery DTP401525(PHR) maximum 15.5 × 27 × 4.2 lies at X±7.75, Y−5.7…21.3, bottom Z=8.3, maximum top 12.5. Carrier floor Z=7.9…8.3 has thermal and C8 reliefs. Tabs have actual inner faces X±8.3: 0.55 mm side gap, or 0.35 mm after a chosen 0.2 mm printing allowance. Do not clamp the pouch. Carrier position/insulating shims establish the bottom datum after measuring the real pack. C8 max 1.45 mm gives 0.85 mm battery gap; subtracting a 0.3 mm board-height and 0.2 mm carrier allowance leaves 0.35 mm. Other low-profile electronics are below the carrier; nominal model/body reviews are not measured assembly results.

TMP390 maximum top Z=6.6 gives a 1.7 mm sensor-to-cell gap. The external 2.032 mm BERGQUIST pad gives 16.3% nominal compression; contact pressure and thermal lag still require hardware tests. PH mated height 5.5 gives lid gap 4.1; SH mated height 6.3 gives lid gap 3.3. Actual wiring and insertion access are reserved in CAD; J3 is serviced with lid removed. J2 intentionally mates inside the cavity toward −Y, so its generic board-edge orientation warning is accepted.

Shutter/pair switches at (−13.5,−7)/(13.5,−4) have 0.2±0.1 mm stroke, 2 mm nominal height. TPU plunger free gap 0.2 mm, stem 6.6 mm, Ø5.8 cap in Ø6.2 lid hole. Power switch at (15,5), 90°, has 1.6 mm travel and a Ø/width 1.3 actuator captured by a 1.7 mm fork. Button compliance, assembly-height adjustment, force and fatigue are prototype tests.

Remote centre in dock Y=−42, back Z=6.8. The stop was corrected to centre Y=−10.9: actual near face −11.7, 0.30 mm from the remote end −12. Rails inner width 40.6 gives 0.3 mm side gaps. A 0.2 mm print allowance leaves only 0.1 mm, so physical sliding/retention must be checked. Magnet top plus cap is Z=4.675, 0.325 mm below nominal PCB bottom. Two remote and two dock magnets plus four phone magnets are external K&J D42 items, retained with insulating caps/adhesive. Screws and magnet positions avoid the pouch/antenna region.

All-layer no-copper antenna strip: X±18, Y−28…−22.9. Detached battery separation is 17.2 mm; closest reserved wire (radius 0.6) is 16.6 mm. In dock, antenna Y−70…−64.9; permitted phone bottom at Y−35±5 yields 24.9 mm to the nearest phone metal. Fifteen millimetres is our engineering target; Ebyte supplies qualitative metal-clearance guidance, not that numerical rule. Use unfilled nonconductive plastic. Phone alignment and docked/detached RF performance require hardware evidence.

Fit evidence: final STL meshes are manifold/NoError; actual tab/stop surfaces were measured in [mesh evidence](../evidence/R3/final-mechanical-mesh-measurements.json). Native tscircuit PCB/3D, cutaway, docked envelopes and four dimensioned sheets were inspected. No physical battery, swelling, wiring, connector, button, slider, magnet, RF or enclosure test has passed.
